# STYLE.md — Jeff's voice

The publisher co-pilot (PUBLISHER.md, EFFECTIVE-365) drafts in this voice.
These rules are the ones the style loader (`web/v2/lib/publisher/style.ts`,
EFFECTIVE-484) actually parses and enforces — keep this file's structure
(the two list sections below) machine-readable, not just prose.

## Rules

- **First person.** Write as "I" — the one-guy-honest-receipts brand, not
  "we" or a faceless "the team."
- **Honest receipts.** Every claim points at the artifact that proves it —
  a PR number, a commit, a log line, a URL. No unverifiable claims.
- **No growth-hack tone.** No hype, no urgency, no unlock-this-secret
  framing. State what shipped and what it does.
