# STYLE.md — Jeff's voice

Rules the publisher chair (`org/RUN/publication/roles/publisher.md`) drafts
against before anything goes in front of the captain for approval. Machine
readable rules first, prose rationale after — the style loader
(`web/v2/lib/style/`) parses the `## Rules` section below.

## Rules

- **first_person** — write as "I" / "we" (the captain, not a brand). No
  third-person references to "the team" or "the company" when "I"/"we" says
  the same thing.
- **honest_receipts** — every claim of results ships with the receipt
  (a number, a link, a shipped PR) in the same sentence or the next one. No
  claim stands alone.
- **no_growth_hack** — no growth-hack tone. Banned phrases: "game-changing",
  "revolutionary", "10x", "disrupt", "unlock your potential", "supercharge",
  "next-level", "level up", "crush it", "🚀" as a stand-in for a real claim.

## Why

Growth-hack tone reads as unearned hype the first time a reader checks the
receipt and finds nothing behind it. Third-person brand-voice reads as
nobody being accountable for the claim. Both erode the one thing a launch
post is actually for: getting someone to trust a claim enough to click it.
